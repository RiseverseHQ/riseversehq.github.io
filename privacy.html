Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

RiseverseHQ is being developed. Account registration is available, while AI chat responses, saved chat history, payments, and TikTok publishing are not currently active features.

1. Information we collect

If you create an account, the email address and authentication information needed to manage that account are processed by Supabase, our authentication and backend service provider. We do not ask you to submit payment-card or banking details on this website.

2. How information is used

Account information is used to register you, authenticate sign-ins, maintain account security, and provide account-related messages such as email confirmation. We do not currently send chat prompts to an AI model or provide a working AI chat service.

3. Service providers

We use Supabase to provide account authentication and backend infrastructure. The website is hosted through GitHub Pages and may load the Supabase JavaScript library from a content-delivery network. These providers process technical information as needed to deliver their services and under their own terms and privacy policies.

4. Storage and security

Authentication sessions may be stored in your browser so that you can remain signed in. We take reasonable steps to protect the project, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Never share your password or verification links.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

Authentication and essential website functions may use browser storage or similar technologies. The current site does not use these features for advertising profiling.

6. Retention and account requests

Account information is retained while needed to operate the account and meet legitimate operational or legal requirements. To ask a question, request account deletion, or raise a privacy concern, contact riseversehq@gmail.com. We may need to verify that a request comes from the account holder.

7. Children

RiseverseHQ is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as the service develops. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised date.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: riseversehq@gmail.com.

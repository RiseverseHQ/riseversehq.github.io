Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

RiseverseHQ is an early-stage service. Account registration and AI chat are available in beta. Conversation saving is being tested. Payments and external publishing integrations are not currently active.

1. Information we collect

If you create an account, the email address and authentication information needed to manage that account are processed by Supabase, our authentication and backend service provider. We do not ask you to submit payment-card or banking details on this website.

2. How information is used

Account information is used to register you, authenticate sign-ins, maintain account security, and provide account-related messages. When you use AI chat, the message you submit and limited recent conversation context are sent securely from our backend to Google Gemini to generate a response. If conversation saving is enabled for your account, messages and replies are stored in the RiseverseHQ Supabase database and associated with your user account. Do not submit passwords, payment details, government identifiers, or other sensitive information.

3. Service providers

We use Supabase for authentication, backend processing, and conversation storage; GitHub Pages for website hosting; and Google Gemini for AI response generation. Chat messages and the context needed to answer them are sent to Google. Google processes that information under its applicable terms and privacy policies. The website may load the Supabase JavaScript library from a content-delivery network. Each provider processes technical information under its own terms and privacy policies.

4. Storage and security

Authentication sessions may be stored in your browser so that you can remain signed in. Saved conversations are stored in Supabase and protected by account-level access controls. We take reasonable steps to protect the project, but no online service can guarantee absolute security. Never share your password or verification links.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

Authentication and essential website functions may use browser storage or similar technologies. The current site does not use these features for advertising profiling.

6. Retention and account requests

Account information and any saved conversation history may be retained while needed to operate the service and meet legitimate operational or legal requirements. To ask a question, request account deletion, or raise a privacy concern, contact riseversehq@gmail.com. We may need to verify that a request comes from the account holder.

7. Children

RiseverseHQ is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as the service develops. The latest version will be published on this page with a revised date.

9. Contact

Privacy questions: riseversehq@gmail.com.
